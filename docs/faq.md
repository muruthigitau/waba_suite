# Questions and fixes

## The typing box has disappeared

You are outside the 24-hour window: the customer has not written to you in the last 24 hours. Send an
approved template with **Send Template Message**. When the customer replies, normal messaging comes
back. This is a WhatsApp rule and cannot be turned off.

## I cannot find a chat

- Check the tab. **All** hides archived chats; look in **Archived**.
- Clear the label filters with the **✕ All** button.
- Clear the search box.
- Check the account. Use **Accounts** to switch numbers — chats belong to the number they arrived on.

## I see no chats at all, or no accounts

Your user is probably not assigned to any active WhatsApp account. Ask an administrator to add you to
the right account. Only active accounts appear.

## A template is missing from the picker

Three usual reasons:

1. it has not been approved by Meta yet;
2. it was approved for a different WhatsApp number — switch accounts to check;
3. it has been created in Meta but not synced into the system. An administrator can sync templates from
   the WhatsApp Templates list in Desk.

## A message says Failed

Something went wrong at WhatsApp's side. Common causes: the number is not on WhatsApp, the customer
blocked the business, the account's token expired, or WhatsApp rejected the content. Ask an
administrator to look at the message in the Desk message list and to check the site's error log.

## My bulk campaign is stuck

- **Draft** means it has not been sent yet: press **Submit**.
- **Queued** and **Pending** mean messages are still going out; large lists take time.
- Press **Check Progress** to refresh the numbers.
- If everything is failing, stop and ask an administrator before creating another campaign — repeating
  a failed send wastes messages and upsets customers.

## My messages are delivered but nobody replies

Delivery is not interest. Check the wording of the template, the time you sent it, and whether the
audience expected to hear from you. Bulk messages to people who did not opt in are usually ignored and
sometimes reported.

## The chatbot is not answering

- Is the chatbot switched on for this account? It can be on for some numbers and off for others.
- Has this chat been handed over to a person? Resume the chatbot when you are finished.
- Is it outside the business hours the chatbot is allowed to answer?
- Is the customer's number on the excluded list?

## Two people answered the same customer

There is no locking; a chat is shared by everyone with access to the account. Use a label such as
*Answered* or *Being handled* to show your colleagues that somebody is on it.

## Can I delete a chat?

No — conversations are records, and deleting them would break the history and any linked documents.
Archive a finished chat instead: it disappears from the main list but stays available in **Archived**.

## Somebody left the team

Ask an administrator to remove them from the WhatsApp account. Their access to that number's chats
ends immediately; the history stays with the account for the remaining team.

## Where do I change read receipts, business hours or away messages?

Those are per-account settings, so an administrator changes them on the WhatsApp Account record in
Desk. See [Administration](administration.md).

## The screen looks different from my colleague's

The chat portal adapts to screen size: on a phone the list and the conversation are separate screens,
and some shortcuts move. Labels, pins, stars and archive are the same everywhere.
