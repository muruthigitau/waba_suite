# Bulk messaging

Bulk messaging lets you send one approved template to many customers at once — a promotion, a payment
reminder, a holiday notice. It always uses the account you are currently working in.

Open **Bulk** from the bottom bar of the chat portal. The screen has two tabs: **Campaigns** and
**Recipient Lists**.

## Step 1: build a recipient list

A recipient list is a saved group of phone numbers. Build it once and reuse it.

1. Open the **Recipient Lists** tab and create a new list.
2. Give it a clear name, for example *October customers*, and optionally a short note.
3. Add people to the list. You can
   - type numbers manually,
   - pick from people you have already chatted with,
   - pick from your saved WhatsApp contacts,
   - pick from your customer contacts in the system.
4. Save.

Lists can be edited later. Before a big send, open the list and check the numbers — a single wrong
prefix wastes messages and can upset customers.

## Step 2: create a campaign

1. Open the **Campaigns** tab and choose **New Campaign**.
2. Give the campaign a name you will recognise later.
3. Choose the recipient list.
4. Choose the template to send. Only approved templates can be used in bulk.
5. Fill in any template values that are the same for everybody, such as your shop name or an offer
   code.
6. If the template carries a header file, attach the file the campaign should send.
7. Press **Create & Send**. The campaign is submitted immediately and cannot be recalled.

## Step 3: follow up

Once submitted, the campaign starts sending in the background and the card shows its progress:

- **Total** — how many recipients are in the campaign
- **Queued** and **Pending** — waiting to go out
- **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read** — how far the messages have got
- **Failed** — messages WhatsApp could not deliver

Press **Check Progress** on a campaign at any time to refresh the numbers. If a campaign could not be
submitted — a permission problem or a dropped connection, for example — it stays in the Campaigns tab
as a draft, and **Submit** on the card sends it. For a longer history and date filters, use the
**Bulk WhatsApp Status** report in Desk ([Reports](reports.md)).

## Good practice

- **Ask first.** Only message people who expect to hear from you; unsolicited bulk messages get numbers
  blocked by WhatsApp.
- **Send to yourself first.** Create a list containing only your own number and run the campaign there
  before sending it to customers.
- **Keep it short.** Templates have a fixed structure, so write the text in the template, not in the
  campaign.
- **Check the template.** Header image, values and buttons come from the template
  ([Templates and flows](templates-and-flows.md)).
- **Watch the failures.** If many messages fail, stop and ask your administrator before trying again.
- **One campaign per idea.** A campaign is not a queue you can keep adding people to; create a new one
  for the next send.

## Who can do this

Everyone on the account can open **Bulk** and prepare a list or a campaign. Sending a campaign needs
the manager permission — if **Submit** refuses with a permission message, ask an administrator to give
you that access.
