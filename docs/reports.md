# Reports

Two reports ship with WhatsApp Suite. Open them from the **Reports** card in the WhatsApp workspace, or
search for their names in Desk.

## Bulk WhatsApp Status

Shows how your campaigns went.

- **Filters:** a date range and, if you want, a campaign status.
- **One row per campaign** with its title, when it was created, how many recipients it had, and how many
  messages were sent, delivered, read and failed.

Use it to answer questions like *did last week's promotion reach people?* and *how many numbers were
wrong?*

Practical notes:

- Your campaigns also show live progress in the chat portal; press **Check Progress** there for the
  latest numbers.
- Messages that were delivered or read are no longer counted as merely *sent*, so the *sent* column
  can look smaller than the recipient count while *delivered* and *read* grow.
- If the *failed* column looks empty but customers say they received nothing, open the campaign's
  messages in the WhatsApp message list in Desk and look at the individual statuses.
- Keep the date range short on a busy account; the report does more work per campaign.

## WhatsApp Conversation Analytics

Shows how much conversation is happening, with whom, and when.

- **Filters:** a date range (required) and, if you want, one account.
- **Summary cards:** Total Chats, New Chats, Incoming Messages, Outgoing Messages, Total Messages.
- **Chart:** message volume per account, in four-hour blocks through the day — useful for staffing.
- **Table:** one row per contact with incoming and outgoing counts, the total, the first and last
  message, and whether the contact was new in the period.

Rows are sorted by the busiest conversation first.

Practical notes:

- The day is split using the system's own time zone, not the time zone of your laptop.
- *New* means the contact was added during the period, not that they wrote for the first time.
- Only conversations that are linked to a saved contact are counted.
- A wide date range on a busy account produces a lot of rows; use days or weeks rather than years.

## Who can see what

Both reports are meant for managers. If a report is missing from your screen, your user role does not
include it — ask an administrator.

## Using the numbers

- Compare campaigns across weeks rather than days; one quiet day is rarely a trend.
- Pair the reports: *Bulk WhatsApp Status* tells you whether messages arrived, *Conversation Analytics*
  tells you whether people replied.
- Look at failures first. A campaign with 20% failures needs a data fix, not better wording.
