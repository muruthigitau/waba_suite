# Getting started

## Sign in

Use the same username and password you use for the rest of the system. Depending on how your site is
set up, you may see a **WhatsApp** tile on the apps screen — click it, or ask your administrator for the
link to the chat portal. The portal opens in your browser and works on phones and tablets too.

## Pick your account

If your company has more than one WhatsApp number, the chats you see always belong to the account
currently selected. Use **Accounts** at the bottom of the chat list to see and switch numbers — the chat
list reloads for the number you choose.

You only see accounts an administrator has given you access to. If the account list is empty, or you
have no chats at all, ask an administrator to add you to the right account.

## Find your way around

- **Left side:** your chat list — everyone you have a conversation with.
- **Right side:** the open conversation, with the message history and the box where you type.
- **Top of the chat list:** search and the filter tabs.
- **Bottom of the chat list:** shortcuts to chats, bulk messages, accounts and logout.
- **Floating + button:** start a conversation with a number that has not written to you yet.

On a phone the list and the conversation are separate screens; use the back arrow to return to the
list.

## Your first conversation

1. Open a chat from the list, or press **+** to start a new one.
2. Type your message and press Enter, or use the attachment button for a photo, video, document or
   audio file.
3. Your message shows a small status marker as it is sent, delivered and read.

If the customer has not written to you in the last 24 hours, the typing box is replaced by a
**Send Template Message** button. This is a WhatsApp rule, not a fault — see
[Conversations](conversations.md#the-24-hour-rule).

## What your administrator sets up

You do not need to do any of this yourself, but it helps to know it exists:

- the WhatsApp Business account and its connection to Meta,
- which team members can see which number,
- the labels available for organising chats,
- which templates are available to send,
- the chatbot and automatic notifications.

Everything else is covered in [Administration](administration.md).
